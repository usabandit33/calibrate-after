Common questions

Is a cheap windshield the same job as glass plus ADAS calibration?

No. Taking the windshield out can change the aim of the camera behind the glass. A cash glass-only quote is a different product from glass plus static and/or dynamic calibration with a printed report.

Do all Phoenix replacements need calibration?

It depends on the vehicle and the manufacturer procedure, not on the shop slogan. Ask them to look up your VIN and name the procedure.

Can a mobile tech calibrate in a Phoenix driveway?

Some procedures are mobile. Some need a level floor and target boards. Some need a marked-lane drive. Ask which of those your car needs and whether that happens the same visit.

Does Arizona insurance automatically cover glass and calibration?

Arizona comprehensive often treats glass as its own rider. Ask your carrier what is covered and ask the shop for two totals: cash and insurance, each with calibration named.

Should I skip calibration to save money?

That is a safety and warranty decision for you and the shop, not this site. If a quote says calibration is not needed, ask them to write the VIN and the manufacturer procedure they checked.
